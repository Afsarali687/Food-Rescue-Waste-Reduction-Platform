Database Architecture & Viva Reference

Aggregation Pipeline Stages Used:
  • $match — Filters non-null timestamps and active document states.
  • $project — Reshapes documents and converts date objects to formatted strings with $dateToString.
  • $group — Computes multi-attribute sums with $sum, $cond, and $ifNull.
  • $sort — Orders results by volume or chronological timeline.
Transaction Safety Alternative on Standalone MongoDB:

In local single-node MongoDB, multi-document transactions require a replica set. To ensure rock-solid consistency, this application uses Optimistic Locking with single-document atomic conditional updates (find_one_and_update) and the Compensating Saga Pattern to roll back reservations if subsequent operations fail.
