Streamlined 3-Step Process

How Food Rescue Works

An efficient, paperless workflow built specifically for rapid turnaround of perishable meals.

01

1. Post Surplus Food

Canteens, caterers, and hostel mess managers publish excess portions with quantity, food category, pickup location, and expiry deadline.

02

2. NGOs Claim Donations

Authorized charities, local food shelters, and volunteer teams review live listings and submit reservation requests for needed portions.

03

3. Pickup & Redistribution

Providers accept requests, pickup is coordinated before the deadline, and meals are delivered fresh to community beneficiaries.
